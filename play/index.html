Nick’s Office

My real office, in pixel art: look all the way around it and click things. The TVs turn on (one plays the last movie I watched, the big one shows my gamerscore), tapes slide off the shelf, the lava lamp bubbles, and Mac and Dennis nap in their cat tower. Most things lead somewhere on the site, and the window turns day into night.

Nick's Nine

A 9-hole HD-2D pixel-art golf game played with my real bag and my real swing. Club distances, misses, and putting all come from my Trackman and X-Golf stats on the Golf page, so the game gets easier as I get better. Nick's ghost plays alongside you, tuned to my real scoring, and the best rounds make the leaderboard.
