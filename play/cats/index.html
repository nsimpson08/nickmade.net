                  How the Cattery works

                  Your cat

                  • Make your cat in the Cat Maker: name, girl or boy, pattern, fur, eyes. One cat per visitor.
                  • Open your cat’s card to feed, pet, play, or move it to a perch, a bed, or the top of a tower.
                  • Keep the recovery code on its card to look after it from another phone or computer.
                  • Come back at least every 30 days (feed, pet or play), or it moves out to live with a family down the street.

                  While you’re away

                  • The cats live their own lives every few hours: they make friends, fall in love, form cliques, start feuds, and nap.
                  • Come back to “While you were away”: a recap of what your cat got up to, and a hello.
                  • Cats sometimes find gifts, from a toy mouse to a rare tiny crown. Open its card to collect them, and dress your cat in the ones it can wear.
                  • Now and then a cat slips out the window for an adventure and comes home with a postcard.
                  • Every cat keeps a diary, written in its own voice.

                  Friends, rivals, and love

                  • Each card’s Feelings shows how that cat feels about every other cat: green for friends, red for rivals, a heart for romance.
                  • Meet a cat sends yours over to someone. Share a treat can patch up a feud, Set up a date nudges two friends toward romance, and Keep apart stops two cats meeting for a day.
                  • Rivals are sometimes caught fighting when you walk in. Lovers cuddle up together.

                  Kittens

                  • When a girl and a boy cat are deeply in love, a cuddle can bring a kitten. It’s rare: about once a month for a devoted couple.
                  • The kitten takes after both parents, grows up over two weeks, and belongs to both owners, who get to name it.

                  Being a good neighbour

                  • Give anyone’s cat a treat and a pet, once a day each. Its owner sees who it was.

                  Tricks

                  • Visit on different days and your cat learns tricks: sit (2 days), high-five (4), spin (7), roll over (10), and a backflip (14). Missing a day only pauses it.

                  The room

                  • Every afternoon from 2 to 4 (Plano time), a big sunbeam pours through the window and the cats pile into it for a nap, unless there’s a storm.
                  • Now and then a thunderstorm rolls in for two hours and everyone hides.
                  • The room dresses up for the seasons: Halloween (October 10 to November 2), winter with a Christmas tree and ornaments to swat (December 1 to January 10), and spring (March 20 to May 31).
                  • The Cattery Times in the header is a weekly front page of the room’s romances, feuds, and scandals.
                  • Take a snapshot of any cat and its best friend from its card to save or share.

                  Getting around

                  • Turn the room with the arrows, zoom with the scroll wheel or a pinch, and drag to look around. History lists everything that has happened and who’s who.
